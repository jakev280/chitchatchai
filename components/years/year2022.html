2022 Highlights

2022 was the year Chit Chat Chai became a true community hub. With free childcare and a welcoming café, women could learn English while feeling supported and included.

The programme combined differentiated classes with practical life skills, and it created space for deep social connection. Learners and volunteers both gained confidence, new friendships, and a stronger sense of purpose.

The year was marked by shared moments in the café, creative learning sessions, and a growing culture of trust, independence, and pride.
