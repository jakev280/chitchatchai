2021 Highlights

In 2021, Chit Chat Chai opened as a warm, supportive space for women learning English. It quickly became about more than classes — it became a place for community, confidence, and connection.

The year balanced tailored language learning with practical, real-world skills like First Aid and digital literacy. Local trips and hands-on activities helped learners feel safer, more capable, and ready to engage with the city.

Supporting women from 11 countries, the project focused on wellbeing and building a genuine sense of belonging through shared learning, shared meals, and shared achievement.
