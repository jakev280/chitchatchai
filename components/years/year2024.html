2024 Highlights

In 2024, Chit Chat Chai deepened its impact as a welcoming community, going beyond traditional language learning. The year blended strengthening confidence with new outdoor experiences and practical support.

Learners took part in creative sessions using art and nature, while volunteers helped build a sense of belonging through collaboration and practical activities.

This year was marked by creativity, connection, and growing independence, with participants feeling more confident in their voice and more rooted in the community.
